# Plumbers: how missed after-hours calls cost you emergency jobs

## The problem: emergencies do not keep office hours

A burst pipe, a failed water heater or a backed-up main line sends a homeowner to the phone at the worst time: 9 PM, a Sunday, the middle of a job you are already on. Whoever answers first usually gets the work. A caller who reaches voicemail has little reason to leave a message and plenty of reasons to dial the next plumber on the list.

## What the numbers say

- **Even the best trade misses a lot.** A vendor analysis of 130,175 calls to 45 home-service contractors found 74.1 percent of calls went unanswered at some point. Plumbers were the best performers and still missed 28.6 percent. Source: [Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/hvac-marketing-attribution-statistics) and [Zyratalk](https://www.zyratalk.com/blog/what-a-missed-call-actually-costs-a-home-service-business/).
- **Benchmarks disagree.** CallRail (January 2025) put home-services missed calls at 14 percent. Invoca (July 2026) found a person answered 52 percent of inbound home-services calls. The gap comes from how each study counts a call. Source: [Stealth Agents summary](https://stealthagents.com/research/home-services-missed-call-revenue-statistics-2026).
- **Emergencies skew late.** Vendor statistics pages report between about 42 percent and 73 percent of plumbing calls arriving outside business hours. These figures could not be traced to an original study, so read them as a range and count your own. Source: [SchedulingKit](https://schedulingkit.com/statistics/plumbing-industry-statistics).

All of these come from companies that sell phone tools, so use the range and trust your own call log most.

## What one lost call costs

HomeAdvisor lists an average burst pipe repair of about $500, with most jobs between $200 and $3,000, and emergency call-out fees of $100 to $300 on top ([HomeAdvisor](https://homeadvisor.com/cost/plumbing/repair-burst-pipe/)). Water damage can add $1,000 to $5,000 depending on the extent ([Inch Calculator](https://www.inchcalculator.com/cost-to-repair-plumbing-leak/)). The arithmetic is simple: missed calls a month, times the share that are new customers, times the share you would book, times your average ticket. Use [our worksheet](/guides/missed-calls-research) with your own numbers.

## Why plumbers miss these calls

1. **You are under a sink.** Hands are busy and the phone is in the truck.
2. **It is after hours.** Nobody is in the office, and the after-hours line goes to voicemail.
3. **Calls bunch up.** A cold snap or a storm sends several emergencies at once, and one phone can only answer one.
4. **Voicemail does not book anything.** Even a callback the next morning arrives after the caller has hired someone else.
