# Kentucky roofers: why leak and estimate calls go to competitors

## The problem: you are on a roof when the phone rings

Roofers work outside, off the phone, for most of the day. Calls arrive in two forms. A leak or storm-damage call comes from someone who wants a response quickly. A replacement-estimate call comes from a homeowner collecting several bids, and in that case the company that responds first often gets the inspection.

## What the numbers say

- **A roof job is worth chasing.** HomeAdvisor's 2026 page (updated June 19, 2026) puts a typical roof repair at about **$1,174**, with most homeowners paying between $395 and $1,967 ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/roofing/repair-a-roof/)). Replacement averages about **$9,528**, with a typical range of $5,869 to $13,220, in HomeAdvisor's article, which carries a 2025 label ([HomeAdvisor](https://www.homeadvisor.com/article.show.Where-does-Your-Money-Go-for-a-Roof-Installation.16862.html)).
- **Homeowners shop around, and roofers are slow.** Roofing software vendors say the average roofing contractor takes about 42 hours to respond to a new lead and that homeowners typically contact three to five companies. We could not trace these to a primary study. Sources: [JobNimbus](https://jobnimbus.com/blog/speed-to-lead-the-simplest-way-to-win-more-roofing-jobs-without-more-leads), [Roofr](https://roofr.com/blog/roofing-sales-metrics-to-track).
- **Phones go unanswered.** Published studies of home-service calls put the unanswered share between about 14 percent and about three in four, depending on who measured and how. They come from companies that sell phone tools, so read the spread as a range ([summary and worksheet](/guides/missed-calls-research)).

A single missed estimate call can cost a replacement worth roughly $9,500, on HomeAdvisor's average. Even a repair is worth more than $1,000.

## The Kentucky market, by county

[Census County Business Patterns (2023)](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/data/api.html) counts **219** roofing contractor establishments with paid employees, and reports a count in only **24** of Kentucky's counties. ## Why roofing calls get missed

1. **Crews are on roofs**, and the owner is on one too.
2. **Storms bunch calls together.** After hail or wind, every roofer in the county gets the same calls on the same day.
3. **Estimate requests need a fast answer.** A caller who reaches voicemail keeps dialing the next company on the list.
4. **Estimates go cold.** A bid sent and never followed up is a bid lost.

## What an answered call has to do in this trade

- **Leaks and storm damage.** Is water coming in now, is any part of the structure unsafe, can the caller place a tarp or move belongings? Capture the address and a callback number, and say what happens next and when.
- **Estimate requests.** Address, roof age if known, the type of work, whether an insurance claim is involved, and a time for an inspection.
- **No pricing or guarantees by phone** unless you have given the person on the phone the words to use.
