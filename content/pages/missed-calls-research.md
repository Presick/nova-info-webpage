# How many calls do home service businesses miss?

## The short answer

Published studies put the share of unanswered home-service calls anywhere from about 14 percent to about three in four. The gap comes from who measured and how they defined a call. Nearly every figure comes from a company that sells phone or call-tracking tools. Read the numbers as a range, and trust your own call log over any average.

## What the studies report

| Study | What it measured | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CallRail, January 2025 | Home-services missed calls | 14 percent missed |
| Invoca, July 2026 | Inbound home-services calls answered by a person | 52 percent answered (65 percent when calls under 15 seconds are excluded) |
| Service Direct (reported by MightyCall) | Calls answered by local home-service companies | 66 percent answered; the same companies estimated they answered 97 percent |
| Vendor analysis of 130,175 calls from 45 contractors | Calls completely unanswered | 74.1 percent; plumbers were best at a 28.6 percent miss rate |
| 411 Locals, 2024 (all industries, 85 businesses) | Calls answered by a live person | 37.8 percent |

Why they differ: some count every ring, some drop short calls, some measure a small group of businesses, and some are marketing material for a product. No study we found gives a reliable figure for how many missed callers try again. One vendor suggests planning with 10 to 20 percent as an assumption, not a measurement.

## Count your own

Use four weeks of your own phone log.

1. Missed calls last month: ___
2. Share of those that were new customers, not repeat customers or spam: ___
3. Share of new-customer calls you would normally book: ___
4. Your average ticket for a booked job: ___

Booked jobs lost per month = (1) x (2) x (3). Revenue at stake = booked jobs lost x (4). Then subtract the callers who would have called back or left a message anyway. Be conservative.

## What to ask any AI receptionist vendor

- What does the full monthly cost include? Is there a setup fee? What does the price become after a trial?
- What happens on an emergency call, who gets contacted, and how fast?
- Does it book into your calendar, or only take a message?
- How does the greeting tell callers it is an AI? How are recordings and transcripts stored and who can see them?
- Does it also place calls or send texts, and how does it get the person's consent?
- Can you hear it before you sign up? Can you cancel with one click?
