# Missed calls for Kentucky plumbing and HVAC contractors: the market by county

## Why this trade is different

Plumbing and HVAC calls often cannot wait: a burst pipe, no heat, a failed air conditioner. A caller who gets voicemail in those moments has little reason to leave a message and plenty of reasons to dial the next company. The published studies on how many home-service calls go unanswered run from about 14 percent to about three in four; we summarize them, and how to count your own, in [How many calls do home service businesses miss?](/guides/missed-calls-research).

## The Kentucky market, by county

[Census County Business Patterns (2023)](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/data/api.html) counts **1,344** plumbing, heating and air-conditioning contractor establishments with paid employees across the 84 Kentucky counties where a count is reported. It does not count owner-operators with no payroll, so the true number of businesses is higher.

Sixteen counties have 20 or more of these establishments, 45 have between 5 and 19, and 23 have fewer than 5. In Northern Kentucky, Boone (48), Kenton (46) and Campbell (39) together hold 133. In a county with only a handful of competitors, being the one that answers matters more than being the cheapest.

## What an answered call has to do in this trade

- **Triage.** Is water still running, is there gas, is there no heat for someone vulnerable? The right first step is often "shut off the main valve" or "call 911", not "leave a message."
- **Dispatch rules.** Who is on call tonight, what is the after-hours rate, and what can wait until morning.
- **Booking.** A time on the calendar, an address, and a number to confirm.

Ask any answering service or AI receptionist exactly what it says to a caller with an active leak or a gas smell, and who it contacts. Kentucky requires [a state license](https://dhbc.ky.gov/Documents/KHBC_Plan%20Submission%20Application%20Guide%2025-0418.pdf) for plumbing and for heating and air-conditioning work, so the person on the phone must not promise a licensed technician unless you have told them to. Recording and AI-voice rules are covered in [Kentucky rules for businesses that let someone else answer the phone](/kentucky/call-rules).
