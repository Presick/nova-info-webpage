## When another option may suit you better

- **Lowest price.** Rosie's lowest tier and some other tools list lower monthly prices. Rosie's lowest tier is reported to take messages without booking or live transfer.
- **A live human on every call.** A live service such as Ruby or Smith.ai's human plans does that, at higher listed prices.
- **Business hours only.** If you only need coverage nine to five, an in-house hire may do, at the cost above.
