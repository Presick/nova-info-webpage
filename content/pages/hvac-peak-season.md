# HVAC missed calls: what they cost in the summer and winter rush

## The problem: everyone's equipment fails at the same time

The first hard freeze and the first heat wave send the same flood of calls to every HVAC company in town. Your techs are on roofs and in attics. The phone rings anyway. A homeowner without heat or cooling calls until someone picks up, and that is the company that gets the job.

## What the numbers say

- **Home-service calls often go unanswered.** A vendor analysis of 130,175 calls to 45 contractors found 74.1 percent of calls went unanswered at some point, with booking rates ranging from 31 percent to 77 percent. Source: [Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/hvac-marketing-attribution-statistics).
- **Benchmarks disagree.** CallRail (January 2025) put home-services missed calls at 14 percent. Invoca (July 2026) found a person answered 52 percent of inbound home-services calls. Source: [Stealth Agents summary](https://stealthagents.com/research/home-services-missed-call-revenue-statistics-2026).
- **A 2024 ServiceTitan study** is reported to show 62 percent of contractor calls going unanswered, per a secondary source. Source: [Beside](https://www.beside.com/blog/ai-receptionist-for-hvac).
- **Peak season is worse.** One vendor says missed-call rates rise to 40 to 50 percent in peak season, citing CallRail and Invoca. We could not confirm that against the originals. Source: [CET Digit](https://www.cetdigit.com/blog/the-refrigerant-leak-in-your-revenue-why-hvac-companies-lose-300000-a-year-at-the-seams-and-how-hubspot-seals-them).

All of these come from companies that sell phone or call-tracking tools. Read the spread as a range, and count your own missed calls over a rush week.

## What one lost call is worth

HomeAdvisor's national average for an HVAC repair is about $340, and Angi's typical repair range is $150 to $650 (2024 figures). A central air conditioner replacement runs $5,000 to $12,000 on Angi's 2024 numbers. Source: [Ainora, citing Angi and HomeAdvisor](https://ainora.lt/blog/hvac-service-call-statistics-2026). A repair call that goes to voicemail can also be a replacement you never get to quote. Use the [worksheet](/guides/missed-calls-research) to put your own numbers on it.

## Why HVAC companies miss calls in the rush

1. **Techs are in the field**, and the office cannot keep up when ten calls land in an hour.
2. **After-hours no-heat calls go to voicemail**, and the caller keeps dialing.
3. **A scheduler on lunch or out sick** means unanswered calls at the busiest time.
4. **Quotes go cold.** A replacement estimate that nobody follows up on is a sale to a competitor.
